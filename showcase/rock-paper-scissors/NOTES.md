Made: a 5.0 s 1080p30 Handkit clip (work/gen.py -> clip.json -> out.mp4), realistic look, motion_blur 4. Two players face each other across the frame: maya (light skin, teal rolled sleeve) from the left edge, omar (dark skin, rust rolled sleeve) from the right, mirror images (maya uses her left hand so both palms face the camera with thumbs up and the shapes read the same way). Both are fists at t=0. Round 1: pumps land 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5, both throw scissors on 2.0 (tie, held). Round 2: back to fists while rising, pumps land 2.5 / 3.0 / 3.5, maya throws paper and omar rock on 4.0; maya's paper flicks up and back (4.28-4.56), omar's rock sinks and tips down (4.35-4.95). Backdrop `--background "#1c1d22" --spotlight 0.5,0.5,0.45,#363a47`, which encodes to the same pixels as showcase/huddle/out.mp4 (corner #1b1c21, spotlight centre #353946).

Timing (measured with `handkit track --fps 120` on each wrist, work/beats.py; shape timing work/shape.py): every pump bottom and both throws land on the beat, first within 3 px of the low at 0.500, 1.000, 1.500, 2.008-2.017, 2.500, 3.000, 3.500, 4.008-4.017 for both hands (the snappy ease's low point is 8 ms later, 25 ms on throws). Throw finger shapes start at t-0.117 and are 90% formed at t-0.06 (index) / t-0.03 (pinky on paper), so the shape is complete as the hand lands.

Time: about 15 minutes wall clock from reading the skill to the final file; three full renders at ~2.5-3.5 min each were most of it (one first pass, two for the backdrop colour).

In the way:
- The huddle backdrop values are not recorded anywhere (its NOTES/gen.py have no player flags), so I sampled its pixels and matched by test-encoding: the player's backdrop comes out ~2 levels off after H.264 (`#1b1c21` in -> `#191a1f` out, `#1d1f24` -> `#1d1e23`), so the flag value is not the colour you see; `#1c1d22` is what lands on #1b1c21.
- No way to time the hand's travel and the finger shape separately in one key, so each throw uses a `pass` waypoint at t-0.12 (still a fist, half way down) and a 0.12 s key for the shape; `cascade` 0.008 keeps all five fingers inside that window (the default spread would add ~0.14 s on paper).
- Library gestures carry their own touch goals (huddle notes), so the fist / peace / open_palm values are copied in as plain joint values; scissors widened (index/middle mcp_abd +16/-14) for a clearer V.
- `check` is clean (no penetrations, pops or framing issues). Nothing under crates/ or starters/ was touched.

What still looks imperfect: the two scissors tips come within about 2% of the frame width of each other at 2.0 (no contact); the fists are seen palm-side (curled fingers and thumb toward camera), which reads as a fist at phone size but is less iconic than a knuckles-forward view; maya playing left-handed is a deliberate framing choice.
