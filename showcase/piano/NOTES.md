Made: a 6 s, 1920x1080 Spider-Verse clip shot from a low three-quarter angle, like a recital. The right hand plays an 18-note arpeggio and the left plays chords that dip the wrist; both end on a final chord. The hands are keyed over a faint halftone keyboard plane with a drop shadow (`gen.py` writes `clip.json`, `bg.py` writes `bg.png`, and ffmpeg composites `hands.mp4` over it).
Time: about 22 min in total: 6 for the first pass and 16 for the revision, which includes two renders of 2.5 and 4.5 min.
In the way: there is no piano pose, so I scripted the presses as joint-angle offsets. Low cameras either showed the elbow stubs or forced the wrists to their limits until the forearms were aimed back and up. The player has no floor or keyboard, so I keyed the hands off a flat backdrop and composited them over a keyboard projected with the clip's camera.
