Made: a 6 s 1080p30 product shot (`out.mp4`). A realistic right hand comes in from the bottom right, hovers over a "Ship it" button on a deploy card, and taps it at 2.52 s. The button presses in, a ripple and sparks go out, it turns green with a check and "Shipped", the progress bar fills, the status goes to Live and a toast slides in. The project is a copy of `starters/fframes-clip` with a restyled `render_frame`; the motion is in `clip.json`.
Time: about 15 minutes. The first `cargo build` took 2.5 minutes even with the starter's `target/` copied in; each render took about 45 s.
In the way: the player can't draw any UI, so I had to copy the starter and write the button in Rust SVG. The UI doesn't know where the fingertip is, so `tip_track.py` samples the clip once per frame (`handkit sample` + `query`) and projects the tip onto the screen to place the touch point and the hand's shadow. The default tap spring made the fingertip slide about 35 px on the button, so I damped it. svgr only takes quoted text inside `<text>`. On the dark UI the hand's shadow is barely visible.
