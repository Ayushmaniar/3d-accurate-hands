Made: an Arcane-look recreation of the "Creation of Adam" fingertip reach (portrait 1080x1920, 8.9 s): a light left hand rises from the lower left, a dark right hand descends from the upper right, they stop a fingertip apart in a pink spotlight and hold with small drifts; clip.json is 6 keys, out.mp4 is the result (cmp.sheet.png / cmp.side.mp4 compare it with the reference).
Took about 10 minutes wall clock, of which ~3 min was the two render passes (motion blur 3, ~0.65 s/frame each).
In the way: the skill documents named people ("adam.left", "people": {...skin}) but the built handkit rejects "people", so per-hand skins were impossible in one clip; I rendered each hand alone (clip_left.json light, clip_right.json dark) over the same backdrop and merged them per pixel with composite.py.
