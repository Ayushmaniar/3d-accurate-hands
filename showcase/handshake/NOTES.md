Made: a 6 s 1080p Handkit clip (clip.json -> out.mp4): an adult (brown skin, navy sleeve) and a child (scale 0.75, light skin, yellow sleeve) shake hands with three pumps, let go, then fist-bump and pull back; generated by work/gen.py.
Time: about 10 minutes wall clock (start to final render), including a 30 s render.
In the way: any person with "scale" != 1 is placed wrong in clips (the wrist lands ~(1-scale) x forearm length off along the forearm, so `query`/`solve` and the player disagree), which also breaks the handshake and fist_bump grip goals between different-size hands (check: grip "off"; the doc says they still solve); worked around by hand-building both contact poses with touch goals plus a small optimizer (work/opt2.py, opt3.py), keying both hands without grips, and pre-correcting every child placement by sampling the clip (work/corr.py); a few brief 2-5 mm overlaps remain in check.

Update: the scale bug above is fixed in the library, so the child's placements are no longer pre-corrected, and the fist bump comes from the fist_bump grip between the two sizes (work/fb_grip_sol.json). The grip touches at the collision capsules, and the skin over the knuckles sits inside them, so the fists come 9 mm closer (12 mm on the hit) for the skin to meet. `check` measures capsules, so it can report contact while the render still shows a gap: look at the frame.
