The lower hand starts higher (screen y 0.90) and its 0.45 s waypoint is lower (0.675), so its path follows the reference: tracked index tip within about 0.01 of the reference at 0.6 s, and at most 0.03 of the frame height ahead at 0.75-0.9 s. - `"arm": "fade"`: the dark forearm's end is in view at the top-right corner; it now fades into the dark backdrop, like the reference arms going into shadow.

How close (honest): the hold (1.4 s to the end) is close for both hands. Differences that remain: the light hand's middle finger sits a little high and its thumb is less visible; the dark hand's thumb is slightly shorter in the picture, and its curled fingers sit a little tighter than the reference's; the light forearm comes in a little steeper from the lower left than in the reference.
