"Snap to switch worlds": a 7 s, 1080x1080 clip where one hand snaps 7 times on an accelerating beat and every snap cuts it into a new look/skin/backdrop (realistic, Spider-Verse, Arcane, clay, toon, dark-skin realistic, Spider-Verse) with a white flash, punch-zoom and a synthesized snap+sub hit, then opens to a palm under a "HANDKIT / one hand. every world." title.
Took about 12 minutes wall clock (about 4.5 of it rendering frames); one motion clip (gen.py -> clip.json) rendered per segment with --look-json, composited and scored in comp.py, encoded with ffmpeg.
What got in the way: the player only renders 16:9 (clip "size" [1080,1920] was ignored), so I cropped to square; the library has snap_ready but no post-snap pose, so I tried 3 joint variants to find a snap that reads; the hand shifts slightly after the first snap (hidden by the cut); first audio mix came out at -21 LUFS and needed a limiter pass.
