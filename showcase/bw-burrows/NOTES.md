Made: a 12 s, 1440x1080, 24 fps black-and-white Handkit recreation of the Burrows/Roberts apron hand dance (out.mp4). It has 22 keys in clip.json, written by gen.py, which places hands in the reference's 4:3 coordinates. It is graded in ffmpeg: centre crop, greyscale, edge fades, vignette and grain.
Time: about 16 min wall clock (02:38-02:54), including about 6 min for the full render (motion_blur 4 on 360 frames) and about 1 min for the B&W grade.
In the way: Handkit has no sleeves, so the forearms end in visible rounded caps (check flags "elbow in view"). I hid them by fading the top and side edges to black. The crossing and folded-hands moments needed manual depth separation and still have 3 short (<=2 frame, <8 mm) mid-move overlaps. The interlaced finger clasp at 2 s was simplified to a loose meeting.
